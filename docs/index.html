FIRST JOB

Send one exact marker

This test sends one small structured generation job through the default route. On a browser-first setup, the explicitly attached AI tab receives the prompt.

first-test.ps1
@'
{"source":"first-test","route":"default","task":"generation","prompt":"Reply with exactly BRIDGE-OK and no other words.","output":{"mode":"text","max_bytes":1024}}
'@ | contextbridge submit --file -
first-test.sh
contextbridge submit --file - <<'JSON'
{"source":"first-test","route":"default","task":"generation","prompt":"Reply with exactly BRIDGE-OK and no other words.","output":{"mode":"text","max_bytes":1024}}
